Lung-RADS v2022 Nodule Assessment

Educational screening nodule categorization • Runs locally in your browser

Not for clinical decision-making. This application implements a limited subset of ACR Lung-RADS v2022 size rules, not the complete system. It does not assess airway nodules, atypical cysts, suspected infection, prior-category stepped management, morphology-specific benign findings, or screening eligibility. Use the official ACR criteria and specialist judgment.

Nodule measurements

Enter mean diameters in millimeters. For part-solid nodules at least 6 mm, the solid component is required. Empty prior fields indicate a baseline measurement; if provided, a 12-month comparison interval is assumed unless specified.

Assessment output

Enter measurements and select “Assess nodules”.

    

    

      
Clinical interpretation and privacy

      
Growth is defined as a strictly greater than 1.5 mm increase in mean diameter over 12 months. An S finding is a modifier and does not replace the numeric category. A growing solid component may change a part-solid nodule's category independently of total diameter.

      
No accounts, analytics, backend calls, or patient records are used. Enter de-identified measurements only; data is processed in browser memory and is not retained by this page. Exported JSON is saved to your device. Use the ACR Lung-RADS reference for the complete criteria.
